# Third-party foundations

Scarlet PC is a Pokémon Scarlet-specific Windows recompilation project maintained by xruanzin.

It uses and modifies third-party open-source components where appropriate. Those components retain their own copyright notices and licenses.

## suyu

Scarlet PC's current AOT runtime/recompiler foundation is based on suyu:

https://github.com/suyu-emu/suyu-main

The validated baseline is suyu v14.1.0 / revision `347fcf91d95cd39cb5c3db50f1b6d827ad699311`.

suyu is licensed under GNU GPL v3 and itself documents its upstream lineage, including Eden and yuzu.

Scarlet PC does not claim authorship of the suyu runtime/recompiler. Scarlet PC's own work includes its Pokémon Scarlet-specific integration, build/package/run tooling, diagnostics, patches and optimizations.

## Game content

Scarlet PC does not distribute Pokémon Scarlet, Nintendo Switch firmware, console keys, or generated game-derived binaries. Users must provide their own legally obtained game files and required console data.
